PS2EXE compiler engine
======================

ps2exe/Invoke-ps2exe.ps1 is the PS2EXE compiler originally written by
Ingo Karstein and reworked (with GUI support) by Markus Scholtes.

Upstream: https://github.com/MScholtes/PS2EXE
License : Microsoft Public License (Ms-PL)
          https://opensource.org/licenses/MS-PL

The interactive studio GUI (PS2EXE-GUI.ps1 and PS2EXE-StudioCore.ps1) is
original work for this repository and calls the bundled engine.
